When a burst pipe is an emergency and your first ten minutes

A burst pipe is always an emergency when it is spraying, pouring or soaking a ceiling. A slow pinhole drip under a sink is urgent but usually not an emergency, since you can close the small shutoff valve beneath the sink and wait for a normal visit. If you cannot tell which you have, treat it as the worse case.

  1. Go straight to the main shutoff valve and turn it off. Do not stop to mop first.
  2. Open a faucet at the lowest point in the house, and then one upstairs, to let trapped water drain and relieve pressure.
  3. If the leak is near outlets, light fixtures or appliances, switch off power at the panel only if you can reach it without stepping in water.
  4. Turn off the water heater. Set an electric heater's breaker to off, and set a gas heater's control to pilot, so the tank is not heated while it drains.
  5. Photograph and film the damage before moving anything. Capture the pipe, the wet areas and the meter.
  6. Start limiting damage by lifting rugs, moving furniture and using towels, buckets and a wet vacuum.
  7. Call the 24/7 line. The plumber can start planning parts and tools while you describe the pipe.

If the ceiling is bulging, keep people out from under it. Water trapped above a ceiling can drop without warning, so stay clear until it is checked.

Finding the main shutoff before you need it

The main shutoff is usually where the water line enters the house. Look near the water heater, in a utility closet, in the garage wall, along the front foundation, or in a crawlspace access. In a rental, the landlord or property manager can tell you if you cannot find it.

Know which type you have. A ball valve has a lever, and a quarter turn across the pipe closes it. A gate valve has a round wheel that needs several full turns clockwise, and old ones can stick or leak at the stem. Test yours once a year so you know it moves.

The valve at the curb meter box belongs to the water system, not to you. If you cannot find or close the house valve, call Union City Public Services at (770) 306-6855 and ask what the city can do. Then call the plumber.

Draining down and documenting for your insurer

After the main is off, draining the system gets water out of the pipes so the break stops leaking. Run the lowest cold tap, then hot taps, and flush toilets once. Leave the faucets open until the plumber tells you otherwise. A plumber can then isolate the failed section and close off the rest so you have water in other parts of the house.

Document before you clean. Take photos and video of the damaged pipe, ruined belongings, wet surfaces and the water meter, and keep receipts for fans, towels and emergency supplies. Insurers expect you to take reasonable steps to stop further damage, and many reimburse receipted temporary measures.

Coverage usually turns on whether the break was sudden. A policy may pay for the resulting water damage but not for repairing the pipe itself, and wear or slow leaks are often excluded. Call your insurer after the water is off and ask what proof they need.

What the January 2024 freeze showed metro Atlanta

In January 2024, a cold snap sent burst pipes and water main breaks across metro Atlanta, according to Atlanta News First coverage. Atlanta Watershed Management crews responded to main breaks, and a master plumber told the station that Georgia homes generally carry little insulation. The same coverage is a reminder that hard freezes can and do happen in Georgia.

The practical lesson is to know where your pipes run. Lines in exterior walls, attics, garages, crawlspaces and outdoor hose bibs are the ones most exposed. Insulate what you can, and make sure you can reach the main shutoff on a cold night. If the line is already frozen but not split, do not thaw it with open flame, and keep the faucet open so water can flow once it melts.

Frozen is not always burst

A frozen pipe often shows no leak until it thaws, at which point the split opens and water pours out. If a tap runs dry in cold weather, shut the main off and check the pipes in outside walls and the crawlspace before the thaw.

Cracked or broken pipe repair, step by step

A cracked pipe is repaired by finding the damaged section, cutting it out, and joining new pipe with a fitting that suits the material. The work starts with isolating the break so the rest of the house can keep water. Then the plumber opens the wall or ceiling only as much as needed, usually a patch of drywall.

Copper repairs often use soldered or press fittings. PEX repairs use crimp, clamp or expansion fittings, and plastic repair couplings let the new piece slip into place. After the new section is in, the system is refilled and checked under normal pressure before anything is closed up.

Where a single break sits in an otherwise sound line, one clean repair is enough. If the same line has had several leaks, the pipe itself is aging. In that case, ask whether a longer section, or the repiping option, makes more sense than a series of small patches.

Cast iron pipe repair when the break is in a drain

Cast iron is a drain and waste material found in older homes, and it fails differently from supply pipe. The inside corrodes, rust nodules build up, and the metal can become brittle and porous. Roots can enter at old joints, and settling ground can crack a line.

A repair cuts out the damaged section and replaces it with PVC or ABS pipe. Shielded rubber couplings with stainless steel bands join the new plastic to the old iron. A cast iron system is often described as lasting decades, commonly 40 to 60 years in real conditions, so a first crack in an old line is a sign to have the rest looked at.

Drain pipe, pipe leak and copper pipe repair in brief

A leaking drain pipe is often a failed joint or a cracked fitting, and it shows when a fixture is draining. A leaking supply pipe is under constant pressure and leaks all the time. Both are repaired by replacing the failed part, and a camera or dye test can confirm which line it is. If you only see damp spots and no spray, the leak may be slow, and leak detection can find it before it becomes a burst.
