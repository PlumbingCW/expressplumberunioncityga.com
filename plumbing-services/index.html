Drains and sewer lines

Start here when water is slow to leave a fixture or a smell is rising from a floor drain. A single slow sink is usually a drain cleaning job. When several fixtures slow down together, the blockage is in the main line, and a camera inspection tells you whether it needs cleaning or repair.

Drain Cleaning

Drain cleaning clears sinks, tubs, floor drains and main lines, and starts with finding where the blockage sits before any cable goes in.

Hydro Jetting

Hydro jetting uses high-pressure water to scour grease, scale and roots from lines that cable work only punches through.

Sewer Line Cleaning

Sewer line cleaning clears the main line that carries every drain in the house out to the city sewer.

Sewer Inspection

Sewer camera inspection shows the inside of the line on video, so the repair is chosen from evidence.

Sewer Line Repair

Sewer line repair fixes broken, bellied, root-invaded and cast iron lines by spot repair, pipe lining or replacement.

Water heaters

A water heater that fails on a cold morning is a common reason people call after midnight. Repair is usually the first answer, and replacement becomes the better value once the tank leaks or the unit is well past the age most tanks last.

Water Heater Repair

Water heater repair covers no hot water, pilot and igniter faults, leaking tanks and failed elements on gas, electric and hybrid units.

Water Heater Replacement

Water heater replacement makes sense when a tank leaks at the seams or repair bills start stacking up against an aging unit.

Water Heater Installation

Water heater installation covers sizing, venting, gas and electric connections, expansion control and a safe discharge for the relief valve.

Tankless Water Heater

Tankless water heater service covers new installs, repairs and the descaling flush that keeps flow steady.

Leaks and pipes

Water that is escaping always costs more the longer it runs. Burst pipes are emergencies. Hidden leaks and slow loss on the buried line need detection first, so the repair opens only what it has to.

Burst Pipe Repair

Burst pipe repair is the 24/7 call for water pouring through a wall, ceiling or floor.

Leak Detection

Leak detection finds hidden water loss behind walls, under slabs and on the buried line, so repairs open as little as possible.

Slab Leak Repair

Slab leak repair locates and fixes water lines under the concrete floor, by spot repair or a reroute overhead.

Water Line Repair

Water line repair fixes the service line between the meter and the house when it leaks or fails.

Repiping

Repiping replaces failing supply lines in older homes, including whole-house work on slab foundations.

Fixtures, installs and commercial

These are the everyday jobs that are usually booked as same-day appointments. They become urgent when a supply line fails or a fixture floods a floor, and the shutoff valve under it is the first thing to close.

Toilet Repair

Toilet repair and installation handles clogs, running tanks, weak flushes, leaks at the base and new toilet installs.

Faucet and Sink Repair

Faucet repair stops drips and leaks at kitchen, bath and utility fixtures, and replaces failed shutoff valves.

Garbage Disposal Repair

Garbage disposal repair clears jams, fixes leaks and replaces dead units with a proper new install.

Plumbing Installation

Plumbing installation covers new fixtures, shutoffs, lines and remodel rough-ins done to code.

Commercial Plumbing

Commercial plumbing serves shops, offices, restaurants and rental properties, including backflow, grease and storm drain work.

Emergency line or same-day appointment

Use the 24/7 line at (770) 214-4545 when water is moving where it should not, when sewage is coming back up, or when a gas water heater smells of gas. Shut off the main water valve first if you can reach it, then call.

Book a same-day appointment when the problem is steady and contained, such as a dripping faucet, a running toilet or a sink that drains slowly. Same-day visits are offered when the schedule allows, and the plumber explains the price before starting.

If you are unsure, call and describe what you see. Dispatch will tell you whether it can wait.
