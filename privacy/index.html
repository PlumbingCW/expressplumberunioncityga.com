Privacy Policy

Last updated October 13, 2026.

Overview

Union City Express Plumbing operates this website and a 24/7 plumbing phone line. This policy explains what information we handle and why. Our address is 4500 Jonesboro Rd, Union City, GA 30291.

Our website has no contact forms, no accounts and no online payments. Most contact happens by phone.

Phone calls

Calls to our line may be recorded for quality and training purposes. When you call, we may collect your name, address, phone number and a description of the plumbing problem so we can send a plumber.

We use call information to dispatch and complete service, to improve quality and to meet legal obligations. We do not sell your personal information.

Website data

Like most sites, our web host may log basic technical data such as IP address, browser type and pages visited. This is used to keep the site secure and working.

This site does not ask you to enter personal details into any page.

Sharing

We share job details with the licensed plumbing professionals who perform the work, and with service providers who help us run the phone line. We may disclose information when the law requires it.

Your choices

You can ask us what information we hold about you, or ask us to delete it where the law allows, by calling (770) 214-4545 or emailing contact@expressplumberunioncityga.com. You may tell the agent at the start of a call if you do not want it recorded.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The current version is always on this page.

Coverage map

Our emergency line covers Union City, Fairburn, South Fulton, Palmetto, College Park, Riverdale and the roads between them.
