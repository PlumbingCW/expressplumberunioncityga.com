What to say when you call

A short, clear description helps dispatch send the right plumber with the right tools.

  1. Say what is leaking, blocked or broken, and in which room.
  2. Give the street address, including the town and ZIP code.
  3. Tell dispatch whether the water can be shut off, and whether anyone is in danger.
  4. Ask for the name of the plumber being sent, so you can look the license up in the Georgia licensee search.
