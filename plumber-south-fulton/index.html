Whose water is it? Check the bill before you phone the utility

Fulton County Water Services states that the county provides drinking water only to North Fulton, north of the Chattahoochee River, and that all other areas are served by the City of Atlanta. For most South Fulton addresses, the water that reaches your meter therefore comes from Atlanta. The same page lists 404-658-7220 for drinking water concerns in south Fulton and 404-612-3163 for sewer concerns, day or night.

The city limits are not a clean circle, though. Union City runs its own water distribution system and buys water from Atlanta, and other neighboring cities can differ as well. If your street sits near one of those borders, the utility printed on your water bill is the one to call, and we can only confirm it by seeing that bill.

Hidden leaks and slow drains in a big, spread-out city

ZIP 30349 holds 30,997 households at about 2.62 people each. More people per house means more showers, laundry loads and flushes, which puts steady load on supply lines and branch drains. A toilet that runs after it fills is the most common quiet leak, and it can add up on the bill long before anything looks wet.

You can do two checks before calling. Put a few drops of food coloring in the toilet tank, wait fifteen minutes without flushing, and look for color in the bowl. Then shut off every fixture, read the meter, and watch the small low-flow dial. If it spins with everything off, water is escaping somewhere. A leak detection visit finds where, using acoustic listening gear and a thermal camera instead of cutting into walls.

Red Piedmont clay is the soil across metro Atlanta. It holds water and shifts when it dries, which can stress buried lines. That is general context, not a map of any one street, so only a locator and a camera can say what is under your yard.

Before we arrive: if you have a burst pipe or active leak in South Fulton

Do these four things while you wait for the crew. They take a few minutes and can save a lot of damage.

Step 1

Find the water shutoff

Look for the meter box near the curb and the house shutoff where the line enters, often in a garage, utility closet or crawlspace. If the water is coming from the street side of the meter, the utility on your bill should be told, and for most of south Fulton that means Atlanta at 404-658-7220.

Step 2

Turn off what water touches

If water reaches outlets or the panel, switch the breaker off only if you can reach it dry. Shut off the water heater at its gas valve or breaker so it does not run dry.

Step 3

Move and capture

Lift rugs and electronics, put towels at doorways, and set buckets under drips. Take photos of the damage for your landlord or insurer before you clean.

Step 4

Give the dispatcher a map point

Say your community, nearest cross street, and the closest park, school or main road such as Old National Highway or Roosevelt Highway. In an 86-square-mile city, that detail gets a crew to the right door.
