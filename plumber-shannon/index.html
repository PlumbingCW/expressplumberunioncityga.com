Where the name Shannon comes from

Shannon Mall opened on August 7, 1980 and closed in November 2010. The building was demolished starting in October 2014, and the site is now Atlanta Metro Studios, a film and television production and distribution complex.

Union City's 2020 plan still refers to the redevelopment area as Union Station, formerly Shannon Mall, and its earlier update names Shannon Parkway for pedestrian improvements. We use the parkway and nearby roads as anchors because the plans name the parkway and the mall rather than a mapped neighborhood boundary.

Condos and townhomes: who fixes what

Shannon Villas is a gated condominium and townhouse community built in 1971 and 1972, according to listing records, with units from 750 to 2,010 square feet. Recent sales in Old Virginia show a median build year of 1974.

In communities like these, the association often owns shared lines and the owner owns what serves only their unit. Your own declaration and bylaws set the actual split, so read them before you book anything.

Plumbing services for condo and townhome owners

  • Water heater in a closet or utility room: repair or replacement. Ask the association whether access, venting or a building rule applies.
  • A drain that backs up in one unit: drain cleaning. Backups in several units point to a shared line, so contact the association before booking.
  • A stain on a ceiling or wall: leak detection helps show whether the source is inside your unit or above it.
  • Slow flushing or a toilet that keeps running: see toilet repair.

Find your shutoff before you need it

In many condos the unit shutoff sits under a sink or in a utility closet. Some buildings also have a stack shutoff that only the association can reach, so ask the manager where it is and who holds the key.

Water, sewer and permits

Water in Union City comes through the City of Atlanta, and Public Services at (770) 306-6855 takes calls about the public system. Inside a building, the association is the first call for anything that touches shared pipes.

Permits for plumbing work go through Community Development at (770) 515-7920. Ask whether your project needs one before work starts.
