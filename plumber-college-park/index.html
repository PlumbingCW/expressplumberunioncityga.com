Why a College Park floor drain backs up

A backup at the lowest drain in a house usually means the main line is blocked, not the fixture. If a toilet gurgles when the washer drains, or two fixtures clog at the same time, the obstruction sits downstream of both branches. That is the point where a cable machine and a camera should replace the plunger.

College Park began as a railroad depot in 1890, and its historic district covers 606 acres with 853 recorded resources. Pipe laid in that period was commonly clay tile or cast iron, and both can crack, shed scale or let roots in after many decades. If a drain has been cleared twice in a year, ask for a sewer camera inspection before paying for a third cleaning, because the footage shows whether the pipe is dirty or failing.

Sewage coming up in the house

Stop running water in the house, since every flush and rinse adds to the pile. Keep children and pets out of the wet area and call (770) 214-4545. Do not pour drain chemicals into a line that is already full, because they sit in the pipe and can burn whoever opens it next.

Sewer line repair on older College Park lots

Once the camera shows a crack, a belly or a root-packed joint, you have three real options. A spot repair digs down and replaces one short section. Lining pulls a resin sleeve through the old pipe, and full replacement trenches a new line from house to connection point.

  • Spot repair suits one damaged section with sound pipe on both sides, and it leaves most of the yard alone.
  • Lining avoids a long trench, but the pipe must be cleaned first and a collapsed or badly offset section cannot be lined.
  • Full replacement costs the most disruption, yet it is the cleaner answer when clay tile is cracked along its whole run.
  • Any option that digs needs a utility locate, so call 811 before the shovel goes in.

Because the pipe may run under a driveway, a shade tree or a historic-district porch, the route matters as much as the method. We lay out the dig path on a drawing with you before work starts, and the price is explained up front. Our sewer line repair page covers the trade-offs in more detail.

Permit rules vary, so ask College Park Building and Inspections through City Hall at 3667 Main Street, main line (404) 767-1537. A permit question answered before the trench is open costs nothing, and one answered after can stop the job.

Before we arrive: if you have a burst pipe or active leak in College Park

A few minutes of action keeps a College Park leak from becoming a flooded floor. Do these in order while you wait for the crew.

Step 1

Find the main shutoff

Most houses have a valve near the water meter at the street or where the line enters the house. Turn it clockwise until it stops, and in an older home test it once now, because stiff valves can break.

Step 2

Open a low tap

Open the lowest faucet in the house to drain pressure from the pipes. Leave it open until the dripping stops.

Step 3

Protect what is dry

Lift rugs, move boxes off the floor and put towels at the base of walls. If water is near an outlet or panel, stay out of that room and leave the power alone.

Step 4

Photograph and note

Take photos of the leak and the damage before cleanup. Write down the meter reading, since both help an insurance conversation later.
